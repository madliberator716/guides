Caramelized Onion & Garlic Spaghetti

Sweet, savory, a little spicy — this one hits every note. Best served with chicken, shrimp, or tofu.

8 oz pasta
~35 min total
Serves 2–3

🛒 Ingredients

  • Pasta of choice 8 oz
  • Large onion, thinly sliced 1
  • Garlic cloves, minced 4
  • Chili crisp 2 Tbsp
  • Cream or coconut milk, room temperature 1 cup
  • Soy sauce — optional 1 Tbsp
  • Fresh parmesan, grated ½ cup
  • Butter 2 Tbsp
  • Olive oil 1 Tbsp
  • Paprika 1 tsp
  • Garlic powder 1 tsp
  • Salt 1 tsp
  • Black pepper 1 tsp
  • Italian seasoning 1 tsp

🔪 Knives & Equipment

  • Chef's knife + cutting board, for onion and garlic
  • Large skillet, for the onions and sauce
  • Large pot, for the pasta

🔪 Ingredient Preparation

  1. Thinly slice the onion
  2. Mince the garlic
  3. Measure out all seasonings (paprika, garlic powder, salt, pepper, Italian seasoning)
  4. Bring the cream or coconut milk to room temperature — this helps it blend in smoothly rather than seizing when it hits the hot pan

🧂 Seasoning & Marinade

No marinade — the dry seasoning blend is bloomed in the pan with the garlic partway through cooking, not applied ahead of time.

🔥 Cooking Method

Medium heat throughout. The onions do the real work here — low and slow in butter and olive oil until deeply golden and jammy, not just softened.

🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prep everything — slice the onion, mince the garlic, measure the seasonings, and bring the cream/coconut milk to room temperature.
  2. Caramelize the onions — heat the butter and olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the sliced onion and cook 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until deeply golden and jammy. If it starts sticking, add a splash of water.
  3. Cook the pasta while the onions caramelize — boil salted water and cook until al dente. Drain, saving some pasta water.
  4. Build the sauce — once the onions are deeply golden, stir in the garlic and all the seasonings. Let bloom about a minute, then add the chili crisp and sauté another minute. Pour in the cream or coconut milk (and soy sauce if using). Simmer a few minutes, then stir in the parmesan.
  5. Toss the pasta in the sauce, adding splashes of reserved pasta water to loosen if it's too thick.
  6. Serve hot with an extra spoonful of chili crisp and fresh parsley if you're feeling fancy.
Don't rush the onions — real caramelization takes the full 15–20 minutes. Turning up the heat to speed it up just browns the outside and leaves the inside raw and sharp.

🥦 Vegetables & Sides

The onion is the vegetable and the backbone of the dish. Pair with chicken, shrimp, or tofu on the side to round it into a full meal.

🔄 Finishing Steps

Parmesan stirred into the warm sauce until melted. Finish with a fresh spoonful of chili crisp on top and parsley for color.

🌮 Serving Suggestions

  • Seared chicken, shrimp, or tofu on the side
  • Extra chili crisp and parmesan at the table
  • Fresh parsley to finish

🚫 Common Mistakes

Rushing the onionsHigh heat browns them without real caramelization. Fix: medium heat, full 15–20 minutes, patience.
Cold cream straight into the panCan cause the sauce to break or seize. Fix: bring it to room temperature first.
Onions sticking/scorchingFix: splash in a little water and scrape up the fond if they start catching.

🧠 Quick Memory Version

Caramelize onions low & slow → bloom garlic + spices + chili crisp → cream + parmesan → toss with pasta
