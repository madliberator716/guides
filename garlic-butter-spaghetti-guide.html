Garlic Butter Spaghetti

Quick, easy, and dangerously good. Buttery garlic heaven with a hint of spice.

8 oz pasta
~25 min total
Serves 2–3

🛒 Ingredients

  • Pasta of choice 8 oz
  • Shallots, thinly sliced 2
  • Garlic cloves, minced 5
  • Chili crisp 2 Tbsp
  • Cream or coconut milk, room temperature 1 cup
  • Soy sauce — optional 1 Tbsp
  • Fresh parmesan, grated ½ cup
  • Butter 2 Tbsp
  • Olive oil 1 Tbsp
  • Paprika 1 tsp
  • Garlic powder 1 tsp
  • Salt 1 tsp
  • Black pepper 1 tsp
  • Oregano 1 tsp

🔪 Knives & Equipment

  • Chef's knife + cutting board, for shallots and garlic
  • Large skillet, for the sauce
  • Large pot, for the pasta

🔪 Ingredient Preparation

  1. Thinly slice the shallots
  2. Mince the garlic — fresh only, not jarred
  3. Measure out all seasonings (paprika, garlic powder, salt, pepper, oregano)
  4. Bring the cream or coconut milk to room temperature so it blends in smoothly

🧂 Seasoning & Marinade

No marinade — this is a fast pan sauce. Seasonings bloom in the pan with the garlic rather than sitting on a protein ahead of time.

🔥 Cooking Method

Medium heat throughout. Shallots first for a softer, sweeter base than a full onion, then garlic added later so it doesn't burn during the longer shallot sauté.

🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prep — thinly slice the shallots and measure out all seasonings. Bring the cream/coconut milk to room temperature.
  2. Sauté the shallots — heat the butter and olive oil in a large skillet over medium. Add the shallots and sauté 8–10 minutes until soft and starting to turn golden.
  3. Cook the pasta while the shallots cook — boil salted water and cook until al dente. Drain, saving some pasta water.
  4. Build the sauce — once the shallots are soft and sweet, stir in the garlic (fresh only) and all the seasonings. Bloom about a minute, then add the chili crisp and give it another quick sauté.
  5. Add cream & cheese — pour in the cream or coconut milk, stir in soy sauce if using, and simmer a few minutes to thicken. Stir in the parmesan.
  6. Toss & serve — toss the pasta in the sauce, adding reserved pasta water as needed for a silky consistency. Serve hot with extra chili crisp, parmesan, and parsley.
Why shallots first, garlic later — shallots need 8–10 minutes to soften and sweeten; garlic only needs about a minute and burns easily, so it goes in near the end.

🥦 Vegetables & Sides

Shallots and garlic are the aromatics here rather than a separate vegetable. Pair with chicken, shrimp, or tofu for a full plate.

🔄 Finishing Steps

Parmesan stirred in until melted and silky. Extra chili crisp and parsley on top just before serving.

🌮 Serving Suggestions

  • Chicken, shrimp, or tofu on the side
  • Extra chili crisp and parmesan at the table
  • Fresh parsley to finish

🚫 Common Mistakes

Adding garlic too earlyIt'll burn and turn bitter before the shallots are done. Fix: garlic goes in after the shallots, not with them.
Skipping the pasta waterSauce ends up too thick or greasy. Fix: always reserve a cup before draining.
Cold cream straight into the hot panCan cause the sauce to break. Fix: bring it to room temperature first.

🧠 Quick Memory Version

Sauté shallots → add garlic + spices + chili crisp → cream + parmesan → toss with pasta
