The 5 settings you're choosing between
ALLOWFull notifications. Lock Screen + Banners + Sounds all on. Reserve this for things that need your attention in real time.
TIME-SENSITIVEInterrupts Focus modes, no sound by default. Good for order updates, DMs, and anything with a short shelf life.
SCHEDULED SUMMARYBatched once or twice a day. iOS collects these and delivers them together instead of one at a time — great for anything you want to see but not be interrupted by.
BADGE ONLYJust the red number on the icon. No lock screen, no banner, no sound. You check it when you open the app.
OFFDon't Allow Notifications. The app can't push anything to you at all.
How to actually change it
1
Settings → Notifications → [app name]Every app's toggle lives in the same place. Alphabetical list, tap in.
2
For SCHEDULED SUMMARY appsLeave "Allow Notifications" on, then turn off "Immediate Delivery" and tap "Scheduled Summary" to add the app. Set summary times under Settings → Notifications → Scheduled Summary (try 8am and 6pm).
3
For BADGE ONLY appsTurn off Lock Screen, Notification Center, Banners, and Sounds — leave "Badges" checked. The app still shows a red dot but never interrupts you.
4
Faster bulk passSettings → Notifications shows a small preview under every app name ("Banners, Sounds" etc.) so you can spot the loud ones without opening each one individually.
5
Even faster: Focus modeRather than reconfiguring 200+ apps individually, set up a Work or Sleep Focus (Settings → Focus) that only allows your Medical/Work group + Phone/Messages through. Everything else queues silently until you check it.

Social & Messaging
13 apps
Split this one in half. Direct messages and calls are TIME-SENSITIVE — a real person is trying to reach you. Everything algorithmic (likes, "X posted," suggested content, streaks) is BADGE ONLY or OFF. Almost every app in this list lets you separate the two inside its own notification settings — do that instead of an all-or-nothing toggle.
Messages / WhatsApp / Telegram / GroupMe / FaceTimeALLOW — these are how actual people reach you, not algorithmic engagement bait.
Instagram / Snapchat / TikTok / X / Bluesky / RedditIn-app, turn off everything except Direct Messages. iOS-side, BADGE ONLY covers what's left.
