🔪 Section 3

Ingredient Preparation

A
Chicken — tender, evenly cooked, good sear
Option 1 (best): Cutlets — slice horizontally through the thick breast into thinner pieces. Option 2: Strips — slice into ¼–½ inch strips.
Critical rule: Always cut against the grain — look for the muscle lines and cut perpendicular to them. This is what makes the chicken tender.
Pro tips: Chill the chicken in the freezer 10–15 min for easier slicing. Pat dry before seasoning for a better sear. Trim excess fat or tendon.
B
Bell pepper (fajita style)
Cut off the top and bottom, stand upright, slice down the sides around the core. Discard seeds and core, lay flat, slice into ¼ inch strips. Aim for uniform strips — even cooking.
C
Onion
Cut in half root to tip, then peel. Slice pole-to-pole (root to tip) into thin strips. This keeps the strips from falling apart and gives better texture.
🧂 Section 4

Seasoning & Marinade

1
Mix and coat
Mix all dry and wet ingredients together, then coat the chicken evenly.
2
Let it sit
Minimum 15–30 min, ideal 2–8 hours in the fridge.

Before cooking, let excess marinade drip off. Too wet = steaming instead of searing.

🔥 Section 5

Cooking Method

Heat Level
Medium-high — 6–7 out of 10.
Preheat Time
5–6 minutes.
Water-Drop Test
Should sizzle and evaporate quickly.
Oil Test
Should shimmer, not instantly smoke.
At 1 lb, this fits in a single batch in a 12" skillet without crowding. If your skillet is smaller, split into 2 batches — crowding steams the chicken instead of searing it.
🍳 Section 6

Step-by-Step Cooking Instructions

1
Setup
Add ¾ Tbsp canola oil to the preheated pan.
2
Sear the chicken
Add chicken in a single layer. Do not touch for 2–3 minutes. Flip, cook 2–3 more minutes.
Looking for: browned edges with a slight char, no excess liquid in the pan, fully cooked with no pink — or 165°F on an instant-read thermometer.
3
Remove and rest
Remove chicken from pan. Rest 5 minutes. Slice thinner if needed, against the grain.
🥦 Section 7

Vegetables & Side Components

Keep heat at medium-high (6–7). Cook the vegetables in the same pan right after the chicken.

A
Onions first
Add a small amount of oil if the pan is dry. Add onions, cook 2–3 min, let sit briefly to develop char.
B
Peppers
Add peppers, cook 4–6 min.
C
Season
Pinch of salt, pinch of chili powder or cumin, squeeze of fresh lime juice at the end.
Goal texture: soft but not mushy, with a slight char on the edges.
🔄 Section 8

Finishing Steps

1
Combine
Add the chicken back into the pan with the vegetables. Toss for 30–60 seconds.
2
Finish
Fresh lime juice, plus an optional splash of soy sauce.

🚫 Section 11

Common Mistakes

Crowding the pan
Cause:Cooking too much chicken at once, or using a pan smaller than 12".
Result:Chicken steams instead of searing — pale, no char.
Fix:Cook in a single layer. Split into batches if your pan is small.
Moving the chicken too early
Cause:Checking or flipping before the full 2–3 minutes.
Result:No crust forms — sticks to the pan and tears.
Fix:Don't touch it for the full 2–3 minutes per side.
Skipping the pat-dry / drip-off step
Cause:Putting chicken straight from the marinade into the pan.
Result:Excess marinade steams the chicken instead of searing it.
Fix:Let excess marinade drip off before it hits the pan.
Heat drifting
Cause:Not adjusting heat as the pan and ingredients change.
Result:Burning (too hot) or no browning (too cool).
Fix:Burning → lower to 5–6. No browning → bump to 7–8 briefly. If using the optional sugar, avoid max heat — it can burn fast.
