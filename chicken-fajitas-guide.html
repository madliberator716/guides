📝 RECIPE GUIDE

Chicken Fajitas

Cast iron, restaurant-style — high heat, no crowding, don't touch early.

2 lb chicken
~30 min total
Cast iron

🛒 Ingredients

Chicken

  • 2 lb chicken breast

Dry seasoning

  • Chili powder 3 tsp
  • Cumin 1½ tsp
  • Paprika (smoked preferred) 1½ tsp
  • Garlic powder 1½ tsp
  • Onion powder 1½ tsp
  • Dried oregano 1 tsp
  • Kosher salt 1 tsp (½–¾ tsp if table salt)
  • Black pepper ½ tsp
  • Cayenne — optional, for heat ½ tsp

Wet ingredients

  • Lime juice 2 Tbsp (~1 medium lime)
  • Canola oil 1½ Tbsp — best for high heat
  • Soy sauce — optional, adds "restaurant" flavor 1½ Tbsp
  • Sugar — optional, helps browning ½ tsp

Vegetables

  • Bell peppers, any color 2
  • Large onion 1

🔪 Knives & Equipment

  • Chef's knife
  • Cutting board
  • Cast iron skillet, 12" — needed for real batches without crowding
  • Tongs
  • Mixing bowl, for marinating

🔪 Ingredient Preparation

Chicken

Goal: tender, evenly cooked, good sear.

  • Option 1 (best): Cutlets — slice horizontally through the thick breast into thinner pieces
  • Option 2: Strips — slice into ¼–½ inch strips
Critical ruleAlways cut against the grain — look for the muscle lines and cut perpendicular to them. This is what makes the chicken tender.
Pro tips — chill the chicken in the freezer 10–15 min for easier slicing. Pat dry before seasoning for a better sear. Trim excess fat/tendon.

Bell peppers (fajita style)

  1. Cut off the top and bottom
  2. Stand upright, slice down the sides around the core
  3. Discard seeds and core, lay flat, slice into ¼ inch strips

Aim for uniform strips — even cooking.

Onions

  1. Cut in half root to tip, then peel
  2. Slice pole-to-pole (root to tip) into thin strips

Slicing pole-to-pole keeps the strips from falling apart and gives better texture.

🧂 Seasoning & Marinade

  1. Mix all dry and wet ingredients together
  2. Coat the chicken evenly
  3. Let sit — minimum 15–30 min, ideal 2–8 hours in the fridge
Before cooking — let excess marinade drip off. Too wet = steaming instead of searing.

🔥 Cooking Method

Heat control

  • Medium-high = 6–7 / 10
  • Preheat the pan 5–6 minutes

Pan readiness test

  • Water drop — should sizzle and evaporate quickly
  • Oil — should shimmer, not instantly smoke

🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

Setup

Add 1 Tbsp canola oil. Use batches — very important for 2 lb of chicken. Cook in 2 batches minimum.

Batch 1

  1. Add chicken in a single layer
  2. Do not touch for 2–3 minutes
  3. Flip, cook 2–3 more minutes
  4. Remove from pan

Batch 2

  1. Add more oil if needed
  2. Repeat the same process
Looking for — browned edges with a slight char, no excess liquid in the pan, fully cooked with no pink.

After cooking

Rest the chicken 5 minutes. Slice thinner if needed, against the grain.

🥦 Vegetables & Sides

Keep heat at medium-high (6–7).

  1. Add a small amount of oil if the pan is dry
  2. Add onions — cook 2–3 min, let sit briefly to develop char
  3. Add peppers — cook 4–6 min

Season veggies

  • Pinch of salt
  • Pinch of chili powder or cumin
  • Squeeze of fresh lime juice at the end

Goal texture: soft but not mushy, with a slight char on the edges.

🔄 Finishing Steps

  1. Add the chicken back into the pan
  2. Toss with the veggies for 30–60 seconds
  3. Finish with fresh lime juice, plus an optional splash of soy sauce

🌮 Serving Suggestions

  • Warm tortillas
  • Cilantro
  • Extra lime
  • Salsa / guacamole

🚫 Common Mistakes

Crowding the panCauses steaming instead of searing. Fix: cook in batches, single layer only.
Moving the chicken too earlyPrevents a crust from forming. Fix: don't touch for the full 2–3 minutes.
Skipping the pat-dry / drip-off stepExcess marinade steams the chicken. Fix: let it drip off before it hits the pan.
Heat driftingBurning → lower to 5–6. No browning → bump to 7–8 briefly. If using the optional sugar, avoid max heat — it can burn fast.
