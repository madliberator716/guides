Viral Buldak Ramen
with Wagyu

Spicy ramen loaded with wagyu steak and jammy marinated eggs. A full production worth every minute.

2 packs ramen
~25 min total
Serves 2

🛒 Ingredients

  • Buldak Carbonara ramen packs 2
  • Wagyu NY strip steak 1
  • Mayo 4 Tbsp
  • Minced garlic 2 Tbsp
  • Egg yolks 2
  • Chili oil 2 Tbsp
  • Salt 1 Tbsp
  • Pepper 1 Tbsp
  • Ramen marinated eggs 2
  • Green onion, to top

The marinated egg method wasn't in the original notes beyond "start the night before" — ask if you want a jammy soy-marinated egg recipe added here.

🔪 Knives & Equipment

  • Medium-high heat pan, for the wagyu
  • Pot, for boiling the noodles
  • Mixing bowl, for the sauce
  • Sharp knife, for slicing the steak
  • Instant-read thermometer — worth it for a wagyu-priced steak

🔪 Ingredient Preparation

  1. Let the wagyu come toward room temperature before searing
  2. Separate the 2 egg yolks, reserving them for the sauce
  3. Have the marinated eggs ready — start these the night before

🧂 Seasoning & Marinade

Season the wagyu simply — salt and pepper only. This is a steak good enough that anything more just gets in the way.

Start the marinated eggs the night before for the best jammy, seasoned yolk by the time you're ready to eat.

🔥 Cooking Method

Medium-high pan sear for the wagyu, flipping every minute for even color. Pulled early and rested — wagyu's high fat content means it carries over more than a leaner cut.

🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Cook the noodles — boil the Buldak noodles according to the package. Save ½ cup of the noodle water before draining.
  2. Mix the sauce — in a bowl, combine both Buldak sauce and powder packets, the mayo, minced garlic, and egg yolks. Stir well.
  3. Add noodle water — stir in the ½ cup reserved noodle water to loosen the sauce.
  4. Cook the wagyu — season with salt and pepper only. Sear in a medium-high pan, flipping every minute, until internal temp reaches 120°F. Rest 5–10 minutes — it will climb to 125–128°F. Slice and set aside.
  5. Assemble & serve — toss the drained noodles in the sauce. Top with chili oil, sliced wagyu, the marinated eggs, and green onion.
Why pull at 120°F — a fatty cut like wagyu keeps cooking hard during the rest. Pulling early and letting carryover heat finish it to 125–128°F keeps it from overshooting into well-done.

🥦 Vegetables & Sides

Green onion is the only produce in this one — it's a rich, protein-forward bowl by design. A quick cucumber salad on the side cuts through the richness if you want something lighter alongside it.

🔄 Finishing Steps

Chili oil drizzled on top for extra heat and shine, sliced wagyu and marinated eggs arranged on top rather than mixed in, so they stay looking good.

🌮 Serving Suggestions

  • Extra chili oil at the table
  • Sliced green onion, extra
  • Toasted sesame seeds, if you have them

🚫 Common Mistakes

Skipping the marinated eggsThey need an overnight head start. Fix: plan ahead — start them the night before.
Overcooking the wagyuIts high fat content means it carries over more than a lean steak. Fix: pull at 120°F and trust the rest.
Skipping the reserved noodle waterThe sauce ends up thick and pasty instead of silky. Fix: don't drain it all — save that ½ cup first.

🧠 Quick Memory Version

Boil noodles → mix sauce + noodle water → sear wagyu to 120°F, rest → toss noodles → top with wagyu, egg, chili oil
