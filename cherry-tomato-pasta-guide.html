Summer Cherry Tomato Pasta

Light, garlicky, lemony pasta with juicy cherry tomatoes and lots of parmesan. So simple, so good.

8 oz pasta
~25 min total
Serves 2–3

🛒 Ingredients

  • Pasta of choice 8 oz
  • Olive oil 1 Tbsp
  • Butter 2 Tbsp
  • Garlic cloves, minced 5
  • Italian seasoning ½ tsp
  • Chili flakes ⅓ tsp
  • Dried basil ½ tsp
  • Black pepper ⅓ tsp
  • Lemon, zested and juiced 1
  • Cherry tomatoes 13 oz
  • Parmesan, grated ½ cup
  • Salt, or to taste ½ tsp
  • Fresh basil or parsley, to finish — optional

🔪 Knives & Equipment

  • Chef's knife + cutting board, for garlic
  • Large pot, for pasta
  • Large pan or skillet, for the sauce
  • Microplane or fine grater, for lemon zest and parmesan

🔪 Ingredient Preparation

  1. Mince the garlic cloves
  2. Zest the lemon first, then juice it — zesting after juicing is much harder
  3. Leave the cherry tomatoes whole — they burst on their own during cooking
  4. Grate the parmesan

🧂 Seasoning & Marinade

No marinade here — the seasoning blend (Italian seasoning, chili flakes, dried basil, black pepper, salt) goes straight into the pan with the tomatoes rather than onto a protein.

🔥 Cooking Method

Medium heat throughout. Olive oil + butter together — butter for richness, olive oil to keep it from browning too fast.

🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta in well-salted boiling water according to package directions. Reserve some pasta water before draining.
  2. Sauté the garlic — heat the olive oil and butter in a large pan over medium heat. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1–2 minutes.
  3. Add seasoning & tomatoes — stir in the Italian seasoning, chili flakes, dried basil, black pepper, and salt. Add the cherry tomatoes and cook until they burst and become saucy, about 5–7 minutes.
  4. Add lemon — stir in the lemon zest and juice.
  5. Toss the pasta — add the drained pasta to the pan and toss together. Splash in reserved pasta water if the sauce needs loosening.
  6. Finish & serve — stir in the parmesan, top with fresh basil or parsley if using, and serve immediately.
Why reserve pasta water — its starch helps the sauce cling to the noodles instead of pooling in the pan.

🥦 Vegetables & Sides

The cherry tomatoes are the built-in vegetable here — no separate side needed. A simple green salad or crusty bread rounds it out if you want more on the plate.

🔄 Finishing Steps

Parmesan stirred in off the heat so it melts into the sauce rather than clumping. Fresh basil or parsley on top for a hit of color and brightness.

🌮 Serving Suggestions

  • Extra parmesan and cracked pepper at the table
  • Crusty bread for mopping up the sauce
  • Extra chili flakes on the side for more heat

🚫 Common Mistakes

Burning the garlicGoes bitter fast in butter. Fix: watch it closely, pull at fragrant, not browned.
Skipping the pasta waterSauce ends up thin or greasy instead of clinging. Fix: always reserve a cup before draining.
Not tasting as you goThe creator "measures by heart" — the listed amounts are a starting point. Fix: adjust salt and lemon to taste at the end.

🧠 Quick Memory Version

Boil pasta → sauté garlic → burst tomatoes → add lemon → toss with pasta + parmesan
